About Me

Who I Am

I'm a Quality Manager and Agile Coach currently at Siemens Advanta, where I own quality across two sub-segments — Audiology and Advantest — while leading the Office of Agile Practices and envisioning the future QMS with GenAI use cases.

Over the past 15+ years, I've worked across Healthcare, Automotive, BFSI, and Telecom sectors, applying frameworks like CMMI, ISO 9001, ISO 27001, ISO 13485, SSAE 18, LEAN, Six Sigma, SAFe, and Scrum to drive real, measurable outcomes.

I'm a natural storyteller with strong communication skills — I believe that great quality culture is built as much through narrative and coaching as it is through process and compliance.
